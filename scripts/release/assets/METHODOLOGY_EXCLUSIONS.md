# Methodology Exclusions

Special-use tracts are excluded from per-capita views by default and can be included via an override, where they are presented with raw counts and crime density rather than per-capita rates. For person-exposure offenses, very small measured exposure denominators are also excluded from per-person rates by default.

## Insufficient Exposure

Too few residents and workers are measured here for a meaningful per-person rate; incident counts and per-square-mile density are still shown.

## County-Proxy Localization

Some state-police, patrol, constable, precinct, and county-named overlap agencies are localized to the coded county as a county-proxy geography; post, precinct, and service-area footprints are not exact county boundaries.

## Motor Vehicle Theft

Motor vehicle theft in special-use tracts, including airports, transit hubs, and other large non-residential areas, is excluded from per-capita rate and index by default. These areas show extreme apparent rates because thefts, often from transient or visitor vehicles such as airport parking, are divided by the few vehicles registered to local households. To analyze these areas, use raw counts or crime density, measured as incidents per square mile.

## Burglary

Burglary in special-use tracts, including airports, parks, and large institutional or commercial-only areas, is excluded from per-capita rate and index by default where the premise-based denominator is too sparse to be meaningful. Across ordinary tracts, commercial premises are weighted by their calibrated burglary exposure so the rate is not distorted by commercial share. For excluded areas, use raw counts or crime density.

## Rape

In special-use tracts with negligible resident and worker population, including parks, undeveloped land, and institutional land, rape per-capita rate and index are excluded because there is no meaningful population denominator. Rape also uses the insufficient-exposure floor: too few residents and workers are measured here for a meaningful per-person rate; incident counts and per-square-mile density are still shown.

## Larceny

In special-use tracts with negligible resident and worker population, including parks, undeveloped land, and institutional land, larceny per-capita rate and index are excluded because there is no meaningful population denominator. Larceny also uses the insufficient-exposure floor: too few residents and workers are measured here for a meaningful per-person rate; incident counts and per-square-mile density are still shown.

## Murder, Robbery, And Aggravated Assault

Murder, robbery, and aggravated assault do not receive a special-use exclusion beyond the standing non-residential household floor. They do use the insufficient-exposure floor: too few residents and workers are measured here for a meaningful per-person rate; incident counts and per-square-mile density are still shown.
