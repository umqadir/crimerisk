## Protocol

Spatial folds remove one admitted feed city from direct allocation, residual training,
the stage-10b prior's burglary premises calibration and uncertainty training, and refit
the larceny finished-share blend without that city. Held-out pooled incidents supply
truth.

Temporal folds truncate the city incident feeds at 2021 or 2023 and score later
incidents. Every other fold input (jurisdiction totals, denominators, covariates,
admission and repair rules, tuned constants) is the 2025 edition's. They are
feed-truncation experiments, not historical forecasts. Their larceny finished-share blend
tables are the ones run `gold_rc4b` recorded for the same truncation, accepted only where
that run's manifest hashes the table and every row matches this run's blend base
(`--recorded-temporal-blend-run gold_rc4b`).

The model fold scores the release candidate on the frozen outside set (E4 panel). Mixture
weights v3, soft shrinkage, the rape triple, exposure ensemble weights, murder K and tau
were selected on this corpus, so it is a tuning corpus for `ours`.

The published 2025.2.1 surface's retained predictions are re-scored on this run's keyed
truth (`baseline_ours`; 730 groups paired, 116 without a matching baseline cell, listed in
`pairing_exclusions.csv`).

Metrics are TVD, log score, Spearman correlation, top-decile capture, exact spatial
Wasserstein skill against the population null, and interval coverage. Confidence
intervals resample cities. Negative skill and losses remain in the output.

## What the evidence covers

Not measured:

- Transfer outside the feed and E4 cities. Feed-city performance is not a bound on
  performance elsewhere.
- Allocation outside the 15 feed cities: 92.9% of block groups on larceny.
- 98.1% of the edition's block groups outside the jurisdiction-panel training hull.
- Direct and benchmark interval calibration under spatial holdout.
- Spatial rape performance beyond one eligible feed city.
- Jurisdiction totals in the data year: the level holdout scores 2024 agency totals only.
- Suburban, small-city and rural allocation beyond the E4 panel.
