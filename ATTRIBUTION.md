# Data sources and attribution

This product publishes modeled, aggregated crime-risk estimates derived from
the sources below. It contains no raw incident records.

- Crime totals: FBI Crime Data Explorer / UCR / NIBRS (public domain).
- Geographies & demographics: U.S. Census Bureau (TIGER/Line, 2020 Census,
  ACS; public domain).
- Workplace population: U.S. Census Bureau LEHD LODES. LODES data are
  provided by the U.S. Census Bureau's Longitudinal Employer-Household
  Dynamics Program.
- Employment scaling: BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (public
  domain).
- Ambient population: Weber, E., Moehl, J., Weston, S., Rose, A.,
  Brelsford, C., & Hauser, T. (2022). LandScan USA 2021 [Data set]. Oak
  Ridge National Laboratory. https://doi.org/10.48690/1527701 (CC BY 4.0).
- Places data: Overture Maps Foundation — Places theme. Data from Meta,
  Microsoft, and other Overture contributors (CDLA-Permissive 2.0); data
  from Foursquare (Apache 2.0); data from AllThePlaces (CC0 1.0).
- Land cover: NLCD (USGS, public domain).
- Incident calibration data: open data portals of New Orleans (CC0), Boulder
  CO (CC0), Louisville KY (PDDL), Gainesville FL (terms:
  data.cityofgainesville.org/terms), Buffalo NY, Los Angeles (city and
  County/LASD), Dallas, Kansas City MO, Cleveland OH, Detroit, Atlanta,
  Charlotte-Mecklenburg, Raleigh, Baton Rouge, Tucson, Colorado Springs,
  Tacoma, Rochester NY, Henderson NV, Fayetteville NC, Ottawa County MI,
  Richmond VA, and the historical portals of the original 13-city corpus.
- Kansas City MO required notice: "This site provides applications using
  data that has been modified for use from its original source,
  www.data.kcmo.gov."
- Cleveland OH: contains information from the City of Cleveland Crime
  Incidents database, which is made available under the Open Database
  License (ODbL).
- Henderson NV source caveat (theirs): crime data provided is not official
  UCR data and may change upon FBI verification.
- City of Richmond and Richmond Police Department names are used solely to
  identify data sources; no endorsement is implied.
- Basemap: OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org); map data © OpenStreetMap
  contributors (ODbL).
- Tacoma WA required notice: this application uses data modified from its
  original source, the City of Tacoma, which makes no warranty as to its
  accuracy, timeliness, or completeness; it is provided "as is."
- Rochester NY data: public domain per the City of Rochester Open Data
  Policy (2020).
